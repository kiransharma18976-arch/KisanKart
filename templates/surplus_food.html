SOCIAL IMPACT

Don't Waste Good Food.

KisanKart helps businesses list usable surplus food so registered NGOs can discover available donations and coordinate collection.

ABOUT THE INITIATIVE

Giving Surplus Food a Meaningful Purpose

Food businesses may sometimes have safe and usable food left over after daily operations. Instead of letting that food go to waste, KisanKart provides a simple digital way to list the surplus food. Registered NGOs can then discover suitable donations and coordinate collection for community distribution.

How Food Donation Works

🏪

Business

Restaurants, canteens and messes identify usable surplus food.

🍱

List Food

The business adds food details, quantity, location and an image.

🤝

NGO

Registered NGOs can view available food and accept suitable donations.

❤️

Community

Accepted food can be coordinated for community distribution.

Who Can Donate?

  • Restaurants and food businesses
  • Canteens and hostel messes
  • Other business buyers with usable surplus food

What Can Be Listed?

  • Fresh, usable surplus cooked food
  • Quantity and unit information
  • Pickup/location details
  • Food description and image

Role of NGOs

  • View available food donations
  • Accept suitable donations
  • Coordinate collection/distribution
  • Give a review after receiving the food

Why It Matters

  • Reduces avoidable food wastage
  • Connects businesses with NGOs
  • Creates a simple digital donation process
  • Supports community food distribution
